Four agents read every page for clarity. Two re-checked the 54 citations that changed since the last check, and 49 of them are right. These are the must-fix items, each with its exact wording now and a proposed fix. The document is unchanged.

  • Citation order and numbering are correct throughout.
  • One reviewer item was dropped. It said “Letting AI tools act is unlikely to stay unique” contradicts the next sentence, but that sentence is the evidence for it.
  • About 70 smaller “should fix” items are in the review files, not listed here.
