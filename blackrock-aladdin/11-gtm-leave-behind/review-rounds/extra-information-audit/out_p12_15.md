## Larger aside-heavy passages or exhibits

- **p12, paragraphs 3 and 4 ("Buyers say ..." and "The client base is large ...").** These are mostly repeats from Parts 1 and 2: the poll, 42 percent, the RFP line, 70 countries, $2.0 billion, the job posting and 17x. Only the $20 billion addressable market, the inference and the two hedges are new. Recommendation: merge them into one short paragraph: "At BlackRock's webinar, buyers named AI and data demands as the top reason to rethink what to build and what to buy 10. The poll did not ask whether clients want AI tools that act on Aladdin. BlackRock sizes Aladdin's addressable market at more than $20 billion and projects API calls across BlackRock and Aladdin to grow 17x by 2030 11. My inference is that clients' AI tools, acting through Studio, could become a large source of those calls. How many clients run their own AI tools is not public."
- **Exhibit 14, Data Cloud strip and its note caveat.** This element is off the exhibit's point. Remove it in the redraw (fix 11, Decision 3) and keep Data Cloud in Exhibit 3. The exhibit also says "how a client's AI tools would connect is not public" three times (title, box and note). Keep only the hatched box.
- **p14 SDK paragraph (between Exhibits 16 and 17).** It is mostly product detail and sits alone between two exhibits. Trim it to two sentences as above, or fold "built for firms with strict IT controls, including gateway routing 29" into the Exhibit 16 Build row and delete the paragraph.
- **p14 text under Exhibit 17.** Half of it reads the table aloud (the list of investment platform names, "LSEG and FactSet come next", the FactSet and MSCI licensed-client line). Keep only what the scores measure, why investment platforms score low with the Arcesium exception, and Studio's 0 against 2 on AI connectors.
- **p15 About this document.** About half repeats p2: the source-type list and the receipts explainer. Choose one home for each. About this document is the right home for method and p2 is the right home for the receipts explainer. Trim this page to the disclaimer, the as-of date, the AI-agent disclosure, a one-sentence source description and the trademark notice.
