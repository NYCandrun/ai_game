The likely recipients are the people quoted in it: Newman, Woodhead, Goel, Sewdass. They will check their own quotes first. What she thinks after reading: "this person spent a day on us with AI, knows what he does not know, and writes like a grown-up." Four places score her product, and the colleague was not in the interview. Hiring managers forward what makes them look smart for finding you. They file what makes their team look bad to the person they forward to. This document is on the line.
