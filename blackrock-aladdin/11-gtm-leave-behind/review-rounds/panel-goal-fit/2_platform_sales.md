**2. Help or hurt as a follow-up?**

Help, on balance. After reading she will think: he did the work, he knows where the gate is, he quotes my colleagues accurately with timestamps, and he hedges where he should. That is rare.

If she forwards it, the reactions split by desk. The Studio GM and engineering will like Exhibits 3, 4, 8 and 9 and will stop on p2's first line, "the layer Copilot runs on." Rosales said tools map to "existing Aladdin API already out in production"; "a couple of thousand" tools against 301 public operations means most Copilot tools sit on internal APIs Studio does not expose. That is the sentence an insider will quote back. Solutions engineering will accept Exhibit 1 and resent Exhibit 2: the caveat says it measures selling model, but the heatmap says 4 out of 16 next to Snowflake's 16, and heatmaps travel without captions. The Copilot team will wince at "GPT-4 function calling" in print, public talk or not. The client business will reject the Janus Henderson line on sight. Investor relations will not enjoy a "0" as the third headline number on p2. Brand will see "Prepared for BlackRock" on a cover set in a stand-in for BLK Fort and read it as a consultant's deliverable, which is the opposite of "whipped up to prep for our calls."

**3. What works against the goal**

- "Prepared for BlackRock" (p1): a deliverable, not notes. It contradicts the note she will get.
- The "0" tile (p2): true, public, and the wrong thing to lead with about her product.
- "The layer Copilot runs on" (p2): overclaims from the Rosales quote.
- Exhibit 2's heatmap: reads as a scorecard despite the caption; the Studio CTA cell is wrong by his own Exhibit 1.
- Finding 3's heading: "works only inside the login" scores a system of record against data vendors.
- "Janus Henderson first" (p15): presumes a reference the client business would not clear.
- AI tells are few. The prose is plain and specific. What shows the method is the uniformity: five identical "What this finding suggests about direction" boxes, the "receipts" vocabulary, 19 pages for something described as whipped up.

Everything a forwarded reader actually reads is on those two pages.

Yes with one change: the body would survive a forward to anyone on the Aladdin floor, but the cover and page 2 currently announce a deliverable with a zero in it, and that is what the forward will be judged on.
