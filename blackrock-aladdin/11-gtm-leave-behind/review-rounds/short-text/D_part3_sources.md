Scope: Part 3 title and intro, every cell and header of Exhibits 17 and 18 and their source notes, the About page, the Sources intro and grade labels, and the descriptive phrasing of Sources entries (not titles or URLs). ## Page 15

### 1. Part 3 title
- **Current:** "Where the research points this role's first six months"
- **Trying to say:** the research suggests six things this role should do in its first six months.
- **Problem:** "points" takes an odd object; at speed the reader looks for "points to" or "points toward". The cover's Part 3 line has the same wording.
- **Rewrite:** "Six directions for this role's first six months"

## The five changes that matter most

1. **Exhibit 17 column header "Why (finding)" becomes "Rests on".** The author's own example; it is the one header a reader hits on every row of the main table.
2. **Exhibit 17 title cut from 20 words to 12:** "Six likely initial directions, with an owner and a measure for each". The "resting on a public fact" idea already lives in the source note.
3. **Rename "what is in the box" everywhere** (Exhibit 17 row 6, Exhibit 18 row 3, page 2 summary) to "summary of what Studio includes". It is the one metaphor in Part 3 the document never defines, and the plain form already appears in the same row.
4. **Row 1 What it involves cell:** give the fragments verbs and put each superscript next to the client it supports, so [51], [3], [50], [45] stop looking like four citations for Royal London. While there, decide whether Nest [52] and PBGC [53] should be cited in that cell.
5. **Make the six Direction labels the same shape** (five nouns plus one imperative today), trim the two that carry a sub-step ("starting with clients whose ...", "for qualified buyers") since the next cell already states it, and give row 2 a concrete object ("beside the API-call projection" rather than "on the API growth story").
