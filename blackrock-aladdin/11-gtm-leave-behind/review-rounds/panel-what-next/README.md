# What-next panel and the new Part 3

Seven readers (strategy, B2B SaaS, finance, management consulting, a market analyst, a technology analyst, a Bloomberg GTM head) answered what the 18-page edition's evidence indicates and what marketing in the Senior PMM role should focus on. `SYNTHESIS.md` consolidates them. From its ranked list the author chose five directions, the RFP answer, a named client, the Copilot plugin-model account, and two worked examples (compliance reporting and private-markets data), and asked for them as a table of what marketing would produce, why, and how to measure it. `apply_part3.py` adds them as Part 3, "Directions to explore", Exhibit 16, with a cover card and a line in the executive summary. The author's note that the stability labels describe the public packages rather than the live APIs is reflected in the wording "the labels the public packages carry".
