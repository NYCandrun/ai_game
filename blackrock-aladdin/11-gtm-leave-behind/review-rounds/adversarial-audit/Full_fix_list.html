Every fix the audit supports, for an airtight v24

This list comes from the full text of all ten reports: the five primary-source audits (A to E), the three conceptual exhibit checks and the two Exhibit 4 checks. It is a list only, and nothing has been changed. The yellow cells are proposed wording, built only from passages the reviewers quoted from the sources. Tier 1 is the thesis. Tier 2 is plain factual errors. Tier 3 is overstatements that a knowledgeable reader would catch. Tier 4 is precision and nits. Section 5 lists evidence the report does not use but could.
