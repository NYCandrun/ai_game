## Recommended corrections
- p05-41: Change "come from field descriptions in those specifications" to "come from descriptions in those specifications". Risk Radar is in API-level info descriptions; only Dashboard is in field descriptions. Source 21 supports only Explore.
- p05-44: Cite the Aladdin Data eXchange page alongside 24 for "feeds ... files out". The APIs page names ATX and ADX but never says feeds, files or events.
- p05-42 (optional): The specs call Aladdin Analytics a "metric source", not an engine.
- p05-43 (optional): Studio is introduced at 4:43, the APIs line is at 4:49, and the sandboxes are at about 5:26.
- p05-46 (optional): The AWS H2 2026 general availability is for US-hosted Aladdin Enterprise clients.
