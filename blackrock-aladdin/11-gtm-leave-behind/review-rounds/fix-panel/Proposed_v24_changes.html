Six reviewers audited my 61-item fix list: a devil's advocate who wanted no changes, a technical product marketer focused on conceptual accuracy, a skeptical Aladdin Studio engineering leader, BlackRock's head of go-to-market (your reader), a competitive-intelligence analyst, and a senior editor. Every recommended wording follows your rules:

  • Plain English.
  • No em-dashes.
  • No semicolons joining sentences.
  • Never "suggested".
  • New text states only the fact.
  • Say things explicitly.

Layout is left to fix later, as you asked, so no wording here was shortened to fit a page.

Panel key: A accept, C accept with change (my recommendation uses the changed wording), R reject, D defer.

Bottom line. Five of six reviewers back an evolved thesis and a v24. The devil's advocate argues for leaving v23 alone and fixing only three lines.
