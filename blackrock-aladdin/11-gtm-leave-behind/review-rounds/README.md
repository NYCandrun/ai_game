# Review rounds for the leave-behind

- `panel/`: six reviewers of the reframed edition (American English, clarity, AI tells in the copy, quality of the findings, a devil's advocate for leaving it unchanged, a consulting-trained MBA), with the brief they followed and the bundle `PANEL_ALL.md`. The agreed items were applied in one pass.
- `readability/`: six identifiers, one per page range, who listed every passage a reader would have to read twice, with plain rewrites (`READABILITY_ALL.md`); `MEANING_CHECK_proposed.md` checked those rewrites for changes in meaning before they were applied (45 of 181 flagged, each with a fix); `MEANING_CHECK_applied.md` compared the document before and after the edit (96 passages, 5 meaning shifts and 5 additions, all corrected).
- `sentence-length/`: four readers, one per section, listed every author sentence of 30 words or more (`long_sentences.json`, the `*_list.md` files) and rewrote each as shorter sentences (`*_rewrites.md`); `MEANING_CHECK_s30.md` compared the document before and after (17 passages, 0 meaning changes). `scan.py` is the sentence counter, `apply.py` the edit script.
- `short-text/`: four readers, one per page range, judged every heading, exhibit title, column header, table cell, callout, stat tile and source note on one test, read once at speed and know what it says (123 items with rewrites); `MEANING_CHECK_titles.md` checked each rewrite before it was applied (94 unchanged in meaning, 28 given a fix, 1 map label left alone); `IMPLIED_NOT_STATED.md` found the four places where two ideas the author wanted implied were stated outright. `APPLIED.md` logs what was done with each item; `MEANING_CHECK_titles_applied.md` compared the document before and after (142 replacements, 3 meaning changes, each fixed by `fix3.py`).
- `forward-references/`: one reader went through the 20 pages in order as a first-time reader and flagged every term, cross-reference or idea that depends on a later page (`FORWARD_REFS.md`: 1 high, 8 medium, 25 low); `APPLIED.md` lists the fixes applied and the one factual correction found on the way (a Databricks connector Exhibit 17 named but the document never established).
- `author-cuts/`: the 63 passages the author highlighted for removal, extracted from the annotated PDF, and the edit script that removed them with the surrounding copy repaired.
- `topic-sentences/`: after the cuts, one reader checked every body paragraph for an opening sentence that frames it; ten paragraphs were given one, with the author's choices recorded.
- `panel-what-next/`: seven readers (strategy, B2B SaaS, finance, management consulting, a market analyst, a technology analyst, a Bloomberg GTM head) answered what the 18-page edition's evidence indicates and what marketing in this role should focus on; `SYNTHESIS.md` consolidates them into a ranked list of six marketing moves, four proposed direction boxes and two candidate lines for the follow-up note.
- Every edit pass is a replayable script under `../../tools/` that asserts each replaced string occurs exactly once.
