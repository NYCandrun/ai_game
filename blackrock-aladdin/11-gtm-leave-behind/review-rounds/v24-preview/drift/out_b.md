## Non-RIGHT items and fixes

1. **Sentence 56 (source 64).** Crockett raised the point as a statement. Nobody asked Smith Garson a question. Fix: replace "asked about clients using BlackRock’s plug-ins in their own copilots" with "after Microsoft’s Adrian Crockett said some clients may want BlackRock’s plug-ins in their own copilots". Keep the superscript after the quote.
2. **Sentence 70 (source 73).** The release says Pictet "will leverage API connectivity", with delivery due by December 2026. Fix: change "and connects its own tools to it by API" to "and plans to connect its own tools to it by API".
3. **Sentence 73 (source 6).** The spec says the PM authorizes the order only in a note on "the PM/Trader split model", and it also has hybrid orders. Fix: change "A portfolio manager authorizes each order before a trader works it" to "In Aladdin’s PM and trader split, a portfolio manager authorizes an order before the trader’s order is created". Keep "and the public order API has no call to authorize one" and the superscript.
4. **Sentence 82 (source 6).** This has the same overstatement as 73, applied to what-if orders. Fix: change it to "In the demo, a portfolio manager would then authorize the orders before a trader works them." The spec then backs the PM step, and the sentence no longer claims that what-if orders go through it.
5. **Sentence 75 (source 6).** OAuth is one of three credential options. Fix: change it to "Studio clients connect with an application key and a credential, such as an OAuth token scoped to each API."
6. **Sentence 83 (source 10).** Sewdass said "if clients want to build ... their secret sauce rebalancing tools ... they can absolutely do so". She did not say they already do. Fix: change it to "BlackRock says clients can build their own rebalancing tools on Aladdin’s APIs."
7. **Sentence 84 (sources 77, 78).** The Client Forum admits "a select group of prospective clients", and Solutions Engineering works with "prospective clients" during the sales process. Neither "opens only after becoming a client", as the Exhibit 15 title and column now say. Fix: rename the column "After qualification or becoming a client" and the title to "what opens only to qualified prospects and clients", as in v23. The other fix is to move these two items out of that column.
