# GTM leave-behind: Aladdin Studio, learned with AI

`Aladdin_Studio_With_AI.pdf` (18 pages) is the document for BlackRock's head of go-to-market. Its frame: this is what an outsider can learn about Aladdin and Aladdin Studio from public sources using AI, this is what it looks like, this is what it says about the direction of BlackRock's business and market, and based on that, these are the quick wins in marketing Studio. It treats enterprise gating as the expected model for this market and is explicit about the limits of public information and machine transcription.

- Part 1, what the public record shows: the product, a trade's path through the APIs, the architecture, the 54-spec catalog, what is out there before a conversation starts and what follows qualification, the access models that coexist in the market, and the five speakers who define Studio on the record.
- Part 2, four findings about direction: public customer proof already exists and is viable; the growth story is API consumption and the whole portfolio; the AI front door is kept inside the platform while data providers market theirs outside, with the case for each side; buyers want a core platform plus their own builds.
- Part 3, quick wins that work inside the B2B model, with owners and measures, and five questions for internal data.
- A 72-entry source register graded P (primary document), S (BlackRock speaker, with minute:second timestamps) and T (third party).

`previous-versions/` holds the first edition ("Aladdin Studio from the outside", 20 pages) and `receipts_check.md` its 60-claim verification. `inputs/` holds the Key Findings documents and "Aladdin, Mapped" that supplied exhibits. `../tools/build_leave_behind_v2.py` rebuilds the HTML; render with `../tools/render_chromium.js`.
