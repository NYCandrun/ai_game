# GTM leave-behind: Aladdin Studio from the outside

`Aladdin_Studio_From_The_Outside.pdf` (18 pages) is the document for BlackRock's head of go-to-market: what a prospect can see of Studio today, with a receipt on every claim. Part 1 explains the product, Part 2 maps the public surface step by step, Part 3 gives six findings one page each, Part 4 is a five-move plan with owners and measures plus the internal data it would need. A 68-entry source register at the back grades every source as a primary document, a BlackRock speaker on the record, or a third-party report.

`receipts_check.md` is an independent check of 60 claims, every quotation and the whole register against the corpus (0 wrong). `inputs/` holds the Key Findings V5.2 document whose exhibits and GTM analysis it builds on; the product material comes from the Briefing and the compendium. `../tools/build_leave_behind.py` regenerates the HTML; render with `../tools/render_chromium.js`.
