# GTM leave-behind: Aladdin Studio, learned with AI

`Aladdin_Studio_With_AI.pdf` (19 pages) is the document for BlackRock's head of go-to-market. Its frame: this is what an outsider can learn about Aladdin and Aladdin Studio from public sources using AI, this is what it looks like, this is what it says about the direction of BlackRock's business and market, and based on that, these are the likely initial directions for marketing Studio. It treats enterprise gating as the expected model for this market and is explicit about the limits of public information and machine transcription.

- Part 1, what the public record shows: first what could not be seen from outside and how providers draw the access line differently, then how BlackRock's speakers define Studio, then what BlackRock sells: the products, a trade's path through the APIs, the architecture, the six pieces and the 54-spec catalog.
- Part 2, four findings about direction: public customer proof already exists and is viable; the growth story is API consumption and the whole portfolio; the AI front door is kept inside the platform while data providers market theirs outside, with the case for each side; buyers want a core platform plus their own builds.
- Part 3, five likely initial directions for marketing Studio in the role's first six months, with owners and measures.
- A 72-entry source register graded P (primary document), S (BlackRock speaker, with minute:second timestamps) and T (third party).
