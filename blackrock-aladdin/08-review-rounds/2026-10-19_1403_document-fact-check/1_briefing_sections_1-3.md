### 1. Contradicted claims (most serious first)

No hard contradictions. Three claims are in tension with the corpus and would be challengeable:

1. **"Over 2,000 citizen developers" (Exhibit 6, "about 2024", "Studio users, including BlackRock's own staff")**. Dave Woodhead, Head of Aladdin Studio Engineering, on the Snowflake podcast (`yt/transcripts/01_From_Analytics_To_Intelligence...md`): "inside of BlackRock we have 3,000 citizen developers and business engineers ... using Python or using R", and "that number has probably peaked". BlackRock-internal alone is 3,000, so the document's 2,000 figure (internal plus clients) is lower than BlackRock's own internal count and the scope differs. Use 3,000 internal and say the client number is not public.

2. **"BlackRock has never named a Studio reference client" / "Outside firms shown in public building on Studio: 1 (Janus Henderson)"**. Not falsified, but BlackRock's own Royal London client-story video (`yt/transcripts2/058_Shaping_Tomorrow_with_Technology.md`, Nov 2024) has the CEO saying "the implementation of Snowflake that we did as part of the overall programme has been really successful". He does not say "Aladdin Data Cloud", so the claim survives on a technicality, but an interviewer who knows the video would push back. Soften to "no client named publicly as building on Studio APIs or the SDK".

3. **Royal London quote attribution (section 3, "What clients say")**. The document attributes the March 2024 statements to the COO and calls the programme "the largest and most complicated the firm had ever run". The corpus has the CEO, Hans Georgeson, who instead stresses it came in "largely on time, if not ahead of plan, and we came in under budget" and that "HSBC as our partners" were the third organisation. The COO press-release quote is not in the corpus; the CEO's "under budget" line is the one BlackRock itself publishes.

Minor: "A BlackRock executive described the point as giving the portfolio manager, the CEO, the chief accounting officer and the controller the same view" (Who buys). The nearest corpus statement (`yt/transcripts/33_Big_Little_Questions_How_can_tech_transform_numbers...md`, Aladdin Accounting) is "Our clients are CFOs, they're controllers, they're heads of performance". Different list; treat the document's wording as unverified paraphrase.
