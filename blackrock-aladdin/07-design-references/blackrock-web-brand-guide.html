Principles

What makes a page look like BlackRock

These are the patterns that repeat across the site. They come from how the stylesheets are built, not from marketing copy.

Black and white do most of the work

Page sections alternate between white and solid black. Text, borders, icons and the primary button are pure black on white, or pure white on black. There are no tinted near-blacks.

Yellow is used in small, important places

Yellow #FFCE00 is the brand accent. It marks the highlighted button in the masthead, the pill-shaped section menu, mid-page promo banners and selected tabs. It always sits behind black type. On the pages measured it covers less than 1% of the screen.

Orange points the way

Orange #FF4713 is never a large surface. It appears as the 16 px eyebrow square above headlines, the 4 px bar under a hovered menu item, and the 16 px arrow after every text link.

Headlines are condensed, tight and bold

All large headings use BLK Fort Condensed Bold with negative letter spacing. Body text, labels and buttons use the regular width BLK Fort.

Grays are warm

Every gray in the BlackRock theme leans slightly yellow-green, from #FAFAF4 to #656560. The light section background is #F5F5EB, not a cool gray.

Large panels float inside the page

The hero is a black panel with 16 px corners, set in from the page edge rather than running full width. Cards are flat white panels with 12 px corners on a warm gray section. There are no drop shadows on the pages measured.

Corners are soft but small

Buttons and inputs have a 4 px radius. Cards and media use 12 px. Aladdin, the sister brand in the same system, sets every radius to zero.

Color

The palette

Tap any swatch to copy its hex value. Contrast ratios against white and black are calculated live so you can check text pairings.

Core

The five brand colors. Black and white carry the layout, yellow is the accent surface, orange marks eyebrows and accent buttons, and pink is a supporting core color.

Share of screen area measured across three pages (corporate home, About Us, and the Investment Institute): white 75%, black 17%, warm gray 7.5%, yellow 0.4%. Orange is too small to register.

Warm neutrals

The gray ramp. Gray 10 is the light section background, Gray 15 is the hairline border, Gray 80 is secondary text.

Functional

Used for market data, form states and alerts. Gain and loss are the colors for positive and negative figures.

Data visualization ramps

Six hues with five steps each. Step 50 is the base. The yellow, orange and pink ramps are built around the core colors.

Accents and flourishes

The small details that repeat

The site uses very little decoration. These are the recurring accents, each rebuilt here from the production CSS. Hover or tap the interactive ones.

Insights

Eyebrow square

A 16 × 16 px square with a 2 px radius and an 8 px gap before the label. Orange on white, black and gray. Black on yellow.

Read the outlook

Sliding underline

Text links and tertiary buttons draw a 3 px bar from left to right, 10 px below the text, in 115 ms. Pressing thickens it to 5 px.

Thickening outline

Secondary buttons have a 1 px border. On hover a 3 px outline is drawn inside the edge, so the button never changes size.

A solid yellow block

Yellow blocks

Yellow appears as a solid block behind black type: a promo banner, a pill menu, a tab or a masthead button. It is never a thin stripe or a gradient.

Accent rule on the left edge

Accent borders

Borders come in 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8 px. The accent border color is orange #FF4713. The 4 px weight marks active navigation and highlighted blocks.
