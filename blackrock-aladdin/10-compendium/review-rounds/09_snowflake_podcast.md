# Manual check 09: Snowflake "Data Cloud Podcast", From Analytics to Intelligence (Woodhead)

## Recording

**Title.** "From Analytics to Intelligence: BlackRock's Journey to Data Productization", an episode of Snowflake's "Data Cloud Podcast" (the Whisper rendering spells it "DataCloud podcast"). Length 27:21 on YouTube (channel "Snowflake Inc.", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Me_H_-E6lSg). Source page: https://www.snowflake.com/en/podcast/from-analytics-to-intelligence--blackrock-s-journey-to-data-prod/. RSS episode date supplied with the assignment: 11 Nov 2025. Neither rendering states a recording or publication date.

**Who speaks.**
- David ("Dave") Woodhead. Introduced in the Whisper rendering's podcast intro as "David Woodhead, head of engineering for Aladdin Studio at BlackRock" [00:08–00:10]; signed off by the host as "Dave Woodhead, managing director and head of engineering for Aladdin Studio at BlackRock" [27:15–27:18 Whisper; YouTube last paragraph "managing director and head of engineering for Aladdin Studio at Black Rockck"]. He says of himself: "I'm a data nerd at heart. Uh I started my career at Micro Strategy" (YouTube; Whisper renders it "I started my career in my strategy" [05:09], an obvious mishearing of MicroStrategy).
- Host: "Dana Gardner, principal analyst at Interarber Solutions" [00:13–00:15 Whisper]. "Interarber" is a mishearing of Interarbor Solutions. Woodhead addresses the host as "Dan" [01:21] and "Dana" (YouTube, "You know, Dana, one of the things I was thinking about"); the closing "Thanks, Anna" (YouTube) / "Thanks, David" (Whisper [27:25]) is a garble of "Thanks, Dana".
- A podcast announcer reads the intro [00:04–00:40] and a Snowflake World Tour advert [27:27–27:55], both only in the Whisper rendering; the cleaned YouTube captions begin at "Welcome to the Data Cloud podcast, Dave" and end at "It's been a pleasure."

**What it is about.** Woodhead explains how Aladdin, "a platform that's been around just over 20 years", moved from an "analytics factory" (the daily "green package" risk report) to an "intelligence factory" in which data drives the investment decision, and how BlackRock's clients asked to use the same software, so "BlackRock pretty quickly entered the fintech business". He defines Aladdin Studio as three pillars (APIs and events; the Aladdin Data Cloud built on Snowflake; developer tools and environments for "business engineering"), describes the Data Cloud's origin around 2019, its purpose ("the language of the portfolio" delivered as governed data products by Snowflake data sharing), and the operating scale ("hundreds of Snowflake accounts", "hundreds of vendors, tens of thousands of pipelines"). He closes with BlackRock's three-fold AI strategy, the "whole portfolio view" of public and private markets data, the 3,000 internal citizen developers whose number "has probably peaked" as natural-language access takes over, and how AI changes engineering work.

Neither rendering states a date. The RSS feed date supplied for this check is 11 Nov 2025. Internal clues are consistent with a recording some time before that but do not fix a year: the host says of the Aladdin Data Cloud "I think you're about five years into that" [13:22]; Woodhead says "technologies like dynamic tables that didn't exist you know 12 months ago" (YouTube; Whisper [20:40–20:42]) and refers to "a 20-year-old process" being the host's phrase [08:07].
