## Recording

Title: "Accelerating Innovation to Transform Investment Management". Category on the transcript header: webinar replay. Source page: https://www.blackrock.com/aladdin/discover/expertise/webinars/accelerating-innovation-to-transform-investment-management. Media file: FT_Live_FullVideo-187.720p.wide.mp4. Who speaks, as the transcript gives them (line 12):
- Moderator: "Kate Dood. I am the Capital Markets Correspondent for the Financial Times in New York". Probable misspelling; the FT's New York capital markets correspondent is Kate Duguid (flagged, not verified from the transcript).

"Accident Investment Managers" is Whisper for AXA Investment Managers (the transcript later says "Axar investment managers" and "AXA I am"). The first name is rendered "Laurent" and "Lawrence" elsewhere in the transcript (lines 14, 32, 66, 96), so "Wilhelm" is almost certainly a mishearing; the surname is unverified.
- "which ties GEPART, Chief Technology Officer, Group Asset Management at Swiss Free". The firm is rendered "Swiss Free", "Swiss vista asset management" (line 18) and "Swiss v asset management" (line 78); with "Group Asset Management" in the title and "100 billion plus assets on a management", this is most likely Swiss Re's Group Asset Management, but the transcript never says it cleanly. The first name is rendered "Matyska" at line 14; name unresolved.
- "Shirley Tvett, Managing Director and Chief Operating Officer at Aladdin BlackRock". Surname rendering uncertain; she is the Aladdin speaker on the panel and gives all of the BlackRock statements below.

What the recording is about: an FT-hosted panel, sponsored by Aladdin by BlackRock, on how the "fourth Industrial Revolution" (the convergence of cloud, AI, blockchain and robotics) is changing investment management, with the Aladdin COO, the AXA IM innovation head and the Swiss group CTO. The BlackRock speaker sets out generative AI use cases (the Aladdin Copilot ecosystem, eFront Copilot, a compliance-rule experiment, data extraction), three AI risk principles, the case for cloud, and the "change alpha" concept; the two clients describe their own AI governance, cloud programmes, technical-debt handling and blockchain experiments. Audience questions cover ESG, build versus buy, trust in generative AI and the limits of automation; the panel closes with each speaker's view of a technology strategy (operational resilience, adaptability, people and culture, process knowledge).

Those inferences are firm for every quotation used above. Proper names are badly rendered: "Kate Dood" (probably Kate Duguid), "Wilhelm Arnold" / "Laurent" / "Lawrence", "which ties GEPART" / "Matyska", "Swiss Free" / "Swiss vista" / "Swiss v", "Accident Investment Managers" / "Axar", "Shirley Tvett", "a lad in" for Aladdin and "JNI" for gen AI. I could not resolve the Swiss firm's name or the two client panellists' names from the transcript alone and did not search outside it, so they are flagged rather than corrected. The recording carries no date; the first-half-of-2024 reading is my inference from the ECB test, the "upcoming regulation" and the Copilot "recently introduced" remark. All quotations above are copied exactly from the transcript file, including its errors.
