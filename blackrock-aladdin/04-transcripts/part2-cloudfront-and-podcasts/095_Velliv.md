JOAKIM ANDERSEN: My name is Joakim Andersen, and I'm a senior analyst in investment operations at Velliv. Velliv is the third largest pension fund in Denmark, and we have approximately 300,000 customers. In terms of alternative investments, we do private equity, real estate, infrastructure, private debt, and sampling. When I started at Velliv back in the day, we didn't have an efficient way of dealing with data collection. With over 90 private equity fund investments, manually data collection seemed comprehensive and almost impossible. However, we wanted to track the risks and the performance of our underlying investments. So we began to look for a partner. eFront really stood out as a pure player in the alternative investments market, with an integrated front to back system that really ensured data quality.

However, the unique selling point for us was eFront's data exchange ecosystem, aka eFront Insight. We knew that data collection for us, and institutional investors in general, was a big hurdle. And so we were convinced that eFront Insight was a sustainable solution for both us and others. eFront Invest has enabled us to do more advanced reporting in terms of transparency of our investments. In addition to that, we are able to support our tax department better in terms of withholding tax. But it also has improved our way of dealing with data collection. We bought eFront Invest for administration, and eFront Insight for monitoring and analysis purposes. We also acquired eFront's data warehouse, which will improve our efficiency in advanced reporting.

The key benefits are improved transparency of our investments, and the possibility to support our tax department in terms of withholding tax. Our aim in investment operations is to deal with the complexity of alternative investments in a comprehensible and efficient way, which eFront really has enabled us to do through their subject matter expertise. We are still on the journey of straight through processing, but we look forward to the journey with eFront. One word I would use to describe eFront is cooperative. eFront is very open minded, and see opportunities rather than limitations.
