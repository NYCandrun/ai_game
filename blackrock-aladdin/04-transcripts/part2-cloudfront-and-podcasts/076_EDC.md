PETER BRADLEY: I'm Peter Bradley. I'm a portfolio manager at Export Development Canada. I've been with EDC for a long time and have been with the investments team for the past eight years. EDC is Canada's export credit agency. We finance and insure Canadian exports around the world. And our goal is to enhance Canada's wealth as a nation. We chose eFront a number of years ago, when we were looking at information systems to meet our private equity needs. And we chose eFront because it was being used by many of our co-investors. And we thought it was a good fit with our business. We've chosen, again, eFront recently as our partner because, in looking at the application and our experience with the company, we felt that it best fit our business needs going forward.

We are currently transitioning from Private Equity Office, which we have had for many years, to FrontInvest. And we have chosen a whole range of modules, including CRM, Deal Flow, the Direct Investment and Fund Investment Portfolio modules, as well as the Accounting and the Reporting and the Dashboarding and the Cash Flow Forecasting and the Lending modules-- are all part of what we've selected. The key benefits of the solution are that it can handle the complexity of our portfolio. It can capture our portfolio. It is well-integrated with Office and is accessible to all of our users, whether they be at home or on the road. And it's a really good fit with the needs that we have in order to be able to serve a diverse group of investment managers who are traveling around the world.

Our relationship with eFront has been excellent. That is part of the reason that we've chosen to continue with our acquisition of FrontInvest. We've had a really good relationship throughout the years. We've had some difficult conversations at times. But they've always been frank and respectful on both sides. And I think our relationship has been strengthened as a result of that. Modules that we could potentially be interested in would include the Value At Risk module, which is interesting to us because it's-- allows us to quantify our risk and better understand some of the risks in the portfolio. The other one would be the Portfolio Monitoring module-- is one that's interesting to us.

It would allow us to better capture the information of the companies that we've invested in. The way I would describe eFront is it's just a great fit. It meets our needs, and it does what we need it to do.
