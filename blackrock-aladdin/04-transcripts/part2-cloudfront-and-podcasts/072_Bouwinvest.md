Bouwinvest at this time is one of the largest independent real estate managers in the Netherlands, working for institutional investments. At the moment we have around 9 billion assets under management. We manage the international real estate mandate for the pension fund of the construction builders, for which we invest in unlisted and listed investments in Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific. And besides that, we manage five Dutch sector funds for third party investors. I think eFront offers the best of breed alternative investment solutions. And we recognized a lot of our day to day business processes in the solutions of eFront.

So with eFront, we have an integrated solution for our portfolio administration, our dashboarding, we use the workflows, and we use the accounting. And furthermore, eFront is accessible for the iPad and in the future there is the possibility to implement the portfolio forecasting tool. At the moment we use eFront Invest for our portfolio administration, and we use eFront Excel and the dashboarding solutions. And besides that, we use eFront Workflow and eFront GL. What is very important to Bouwinvest is the reliability and robustness of the system, because this is crucial for our day to day business. Besides that, it is very important that we have a single point of entry of our data for workflow systems, which ensures the reliability of the data quality.

I think a third point for us which is important, is the reporting solution at eFront, which is quite good, and use of the dashboards. And we had some good reference calls with peers in our industry, which were already using eFront. On the user side, we are very content with eFront. Product development is very important for eFront, and they are developing new products on a high pace. Given our philosophy of outsourcing our IT controls-- IT controls are important for us, and this has been challenging in the past. We are currently looking for solutions for the exchange of big data with eFront in our business-- the real estate business.
