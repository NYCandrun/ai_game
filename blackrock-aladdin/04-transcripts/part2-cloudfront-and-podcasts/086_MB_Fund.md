GUS TAMBAKIS: My name is Gus Tambakis and I am the director of new business development and client relations for MB Fund Administration. We are a private equity fund administrator for firms that are usually underneath a billion dollars in AUM, and we service all aspects of their back office needs. The market challenges that we see, especially for our clients, is the amount of data that's needed to be produced for our clients, which in turn is for their LPs. So we see that having the ability to aggregate that data for them in something that's precise and concise makes our clients happy, which in turn makes their LPs happy.

We chose eFront because of the ability for eFront to configure the system for our needs, which in turn was able for us to customize reporting for our clients. The ability of eFront was far superior to anything else out in the market at that point in time, and we've been nothing but happy with the service so far. Our solution with eFront consists of Front Invests and we use it forwards and backwards. We have the general ledger, we have the CRM tool, we have the analytics, we have the queries, we have the dashboards, we use the fundraising front Excel, and deal flow modules. We use a slow and methodical approach to implementing our service, so it took a little bit longer time because we wanted to make sure that we were doing the right thing by our clients, and eFront was willing to work with us on our timetable instead of pushing the issue.

Currently, we're looking to possibly bring in Investment Café and to make a seamless solution for everything needed in our fund administration business. We've been with eFront since 2013. We've had nothing but really a great success story with them, and we're very appreciative of what they do for us and we look forward to working with them in the future. [MUSIC PLAYING]
