I am Roban Ludo, and I am a former international and various. Six marks, six M, is a no-frize alternative investment fund manager, specialized for private equity, venture capital and crypto funds. Our expertise to manage crypto funds within a regulated environment is really a good example. The first one is the diversification into new asset classes. Asset manager are seeking from new ways to diversify their portfolios and to optimize their returns. A great example is crypto assets. Over the past few years, we have seen a growing interest from institutional investors to get an exposure to crypto assets. The second trend we see is liquidity for alternative funds.

Besides the trend of the retailization, the liquidity for alternative funds is a key challenge. We closely worked with Black Hawk and Ephraim teams to manage both open-ended funds with subscription and redemption and close-ended funds with capital call, return of capital and distribution within the same fund- accounting system and transformation system. On the asset side, we successfully integrated crypto assets with multi-digit assets, vesting mechanism and stacking of tokens within the asset. I expect to see an area adoption of crypto assets in the financial sector. I'm confident we will see more institutional players offering exposure to crypto either de-weight or in-de-weight exposure to their clients over the next few years.

I believe that crypto assets will become a standard asset class in the world of alternative investments.
