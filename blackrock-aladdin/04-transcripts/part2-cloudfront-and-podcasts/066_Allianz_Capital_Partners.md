[MUSIC PLAYING] DANIEL GREGOR: My name is Daniel Gregor. I work at Allianz Capital Partners. There I'm heading the private equity finance team, the reporting team, and the business intelligence team. Allianz Capital Partners is the alternative asset platform for Allianz, so we invest in different asset classes, which is private equity, fund business, then we have infrastructure, direct and fund business, and we have renewable energy. We chose eFront as a partner back in 2005. At that time we had already 80 funds signed, and actually, in the beginning we had Excel as our database, followed by Access database, and after 80 funds it was too difficult to manage data.

So there was a decision to buy something like a real, proper alternative asset database, and then the decision was for eFront. And in reflection, I think we took the right path. Since 2005, we use eFront Invest for our private equity business. Back office-wise, the portfolio monitoring module, of course. For the investment team, actually it is tracking the invested funds, non-invested funds, so we have our own benchmark solution within eFront. We have the cash flows distribution, and image capturing is something which we very often use. And beginning of 2009, we actually was merged with renewable energy, so we bought the direct investment module to capture their data as well.

And in 2013, we also captured the data for our infrastructure investment team. So now we have all business lines within eFront, and have a comprehensive database for all our needs. It's hard to say which is the key benefit. I mean, one benefit-- a great benefit-- is actually that we have one single point of truth, and the second one would be that we have, actually, the right numbers in place when we need it. So we have very hard deadlines, reporting-wise, so we need to submit the data x plus 1 to the group. So therefore, it's very important that we have all the data at one point.

Of course, the efficiency within the company is much higher than if you wouldn't have eFront. Another benefit of eFront for us is that the investment team uses the same data like the finance team, so actually there's a direct flow from the investment team and they look at the fund pre-decision, then we assign the fund, and as the fund is assigned, actually it goes directly to the finance team and it stays in one solution. The last module we bought was actually eFront investor cloud. Why this is the case? I mean, we have almost 400 funds and for our administration team, it's horrible. A lot of work because GPs are not willing to provide us data electronically.

To capture all the data of all portfolio companies, cash flows, capital accounts, there's a lot of manual work. And therefore we decided actually, to outsource this to eFront, to a team with full-time employees, to help us to administrate our portfolio, and we are looking forward as we can focus more on our core business. eFront-- it's a single point of truth within Allianz Capital Partners.
