Tired of generating individually customized reporting for all of your investors, wasting valuable resources to produce countless templates and data sheets that in the end prove unsatisfactory, you are not alone. Managing investor relations is consistently ranked as a major challenge for general partners around the world. That's why EFRNT, the industry leader in alternative investment technologies, created a solution to digitize private market data exchanges between general and limited partners, called EFRNT Insight. As general partners upload their data, it instantly becomes available within the platform. Embedded checks ensure that submitted data is accurate and complete. And with more than 100 dedicated data analysts on hand, we are always available to help with data submission and analysis.

General partners can leverage EFRNT Insight's specialized and vestive viewing features in order to review data using the exact same analytical charts, benchmarks, and tools as their investors, giving them greater understanding into how limited partners both view and interpret information. With hundreds of pre-built charts and data views, general partners can quickly gain unique insights into their funds and investments. Thanks to immediate feedback on data completion and the inclusion of over 1,000 public indices, general partners can effortlessly benchmark their performance, all through one centralized platform. With the click of a button, they can then generate a variety of investor reporting templates enabling them to easily and consistently respond to multiple investor requests using a single source of truth.
