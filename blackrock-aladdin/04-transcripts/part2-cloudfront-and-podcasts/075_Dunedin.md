KARAN DARROCH: My name is Karan Darroch. I am a finance and operations manager with Dunedin LLP. Dunedin is a private equity manager based in Edinburgh and in London. We are a buyout house typically working in the low to lower end of the mid-market deal size, targeting UK companies. We implemented eFront back in 2010, and at the time, we selected eFront as a partner as it offered the best end-to-end solution for our business. We use eFront Invest and we use it for CRM, for deal flow management, and for fund management. So we use the CRM module to keep track of all of our relationships with all of our investors, our clients, and with our contacts.

We record all activities and use that as a really effective tool. We use the deal flow management to keep track of all the deals that we're working on and to keep track of our work in progress and to manage the projects within the teams. And we use fund management to keep our investor records, to keep our fund records, and to report to our investors through the tool on a quarterly or ad hoc basis. I think the key benefits of the system are the efficiencies of having all of the information in one place, one single source of information, particularly in relation to contacts and investor records.

And if you are changing something, you're changing at once and it means that it's changed throughout for all the different users. I think the efficiency of reporting from the system is great. We use the automated reporting with the other notices functionality, and it means that you can send tailored responses to investors from one place very quickly and easily, which makes my job a lot easier. I'm very satisfied with the relationship with eFront. I think it's something we've continued to develop over the years, particularly since I joined Dunedin and have taken ownership of managing the tool and managing the relationship. One of the things that I think has really improved in recent years has been the level of service and support, particularly since the implementation of the Belgrade team.

I think the team are very responsive. One of the things that I like is that they will provide notes to help me learn how to do things, know they appreciate that we want to be quite self-serving. So I've had to learn a bit of how to do configuration, and rather than the team just doing it for me, they'll give me instructions that I can keep as to how to do it, which means I can go back and refer to those, which I think is great. As a separate aside, we are a GP who is compliant with Insight. We are reporting via Insight on a quarterly basis and I am a big fan.

I think that it is a great tool. Initially, there was a lot of work involved in having to get that data into the system, and it really was driven by one of our LPs signing up to requesting reporting via Insight. We now have a number of LPs reporting in that way, and actually, it's great because it means I can upload data once a quarter, know I have a set template and format. It's very efficient to be able to do and it means I can provide the granular level of detail that investors require fairly easily. It also has a GP function where I can look at the performance of our funds, which is nice.

And at the moment, there's no cost to the GP of having to use that, which I think is great. And the actual interface of it is user friendly, so it's something that I can share throughout the business to others who are perhaps not as tech savvy and are just looking for a high level overview of the funds. So yeah, I think it's a good product and I'm looking forward to seeing it developing a bit further. If I had to use a phrase to describe eFront, I would say constantly evolving. The business has changed a lot over the years that I've been involved and I'm interested to see where the next part of your journey goes, particularly under your new ownership.
