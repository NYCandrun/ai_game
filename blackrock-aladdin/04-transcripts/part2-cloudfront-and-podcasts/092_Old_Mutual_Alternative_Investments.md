Old Mutual Alternative Investments is the largest private manager of alternative assets on the African continent. We manage about $5 billion in a range of impact funds, private equity, and infrastructure investments. We have 21 funds, 160 portfolio companies, and 70 investment professionals spread across South, East, and West Africa. In 2012, we embarked upon a journey to select an operating platform to suit our unique needs. We understood that this would be a very strategic and significant investment for us, and we therefore took our time with the selection process. eFront, at the time, was the market leader and was growing at an exponential rate. They were, and still are, highly innovative in their product evolution.

As a private equity portfolio company themselves, they also understand the value of a long-term partnership. So after an extensive selection process, we chose eFront as our preferred and trusted partner. We use eFront as an end-to-end operating platform. We use it for deal flow, managing the investment process, compliance, general ledger, and client reporting. The single biggest benefit for us is the fact that we now have a single version of the truth. All the information for all our funds and all our portfolio companies is now in one place, accessible to the entire business. We have been partners with eFront for six years, and we're looking forward to a successful ongoing relationship.
