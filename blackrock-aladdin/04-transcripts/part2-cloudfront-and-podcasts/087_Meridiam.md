STEPHANIA VASILIEVA: My name is Stephania Vasilieva. I'm an analyst at the asset management team at Meridiam. Meridiam is a long-term investment fund specializing in greenfield infrastructure. We've been using eFront for three years now at Meridiam. The two solutions we've been using are eFront Office as well as eFront Investment Café. eFront Office is being used by the asset management team to really have one source, one database where we have all of our project information for all of our stages. And then we use the Investment Café solution by the investment team, which really helps them collaborate with our investors. Whether it be for reporting, sending out capital call notices, fundraising, documents, or marketing, eFront Investment Café just really helps us share information with our investors.

The key benefits of the eFront solutions is really that it's consistent across all boards. Meridiam is a global company. We have offices in Africa and Europe, in the US, and they all have different sizes, and so it's nice having one place where everything is the same and we all follow the same guidelines as to how information is filled out into eFront. Regarding Investment Café, it's nice because again, it makes it really easy for all of the investors to fill out one format, one report, and to have one place where we put in everything. The collaboration with eFront is really outstanding. Every time I talk to them, every time I have questions, they're really on top of things.

They want to help me, they want me to have my answer, whether it be something very small that maybe I should have known because it's more internal to Meridiam's guidelines or whether it be really a technical thing that I just don't understand because it's out of my expertise, eFront has really been super professional and really nice with helping me and making me understand what is happening. If I had to describe eFront in one word, I would say standardized, as that is something that is very important to Meridiam as a global company. With having so many people in so many offices across the globe, it's important for everyone to follow the same sets of guidelines.
