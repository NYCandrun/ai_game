I'm Stephen, I'm head of the Administration Services at ADEPA. ADEPA is a third-party management company and funded administrator with headquarters here in Luxembourg and with offices in Italy, Spain and Chile. Basically, we try to offer comprehensive service, a one-stop shop, including portfolio monitoring, funded administration and investor services, and both for liquid traditional investments and for alternative investment funds. The main train that we see is actually that most of the investment managers want to outsource their back-office operations and kind of concentrate on what they do best, on their core. That is really to make investment decisions to take care of their investors. Another strong trend is to try to improve the efficiency of the operation through automation and through data centralization.

If front is very much aligned with this trend, actually, because it allows us to offer a one-stop solution to clients, to automate all the processes that we have that we previously had in lots of different places. And at the same time, it still allows us to have the flexibility to offer customized services to our clients. Our needs evolved a lot because we started being quite a small company and we have now acquired a size where we couldn't do all the, let's say, manual work that we were doing before. We also expanded some of the activities, for example, portfolio monitoring that we were not doing before.

And this is really where we realized that we needed a very strong technology to be able to support the service that we want to give to our clients with the level of quality that we want to give. If front helps us streamline our operations, because it allows us to keep all data in one place and to have kind of the same process across different jurisdictions. So let's say we have a client that has a fund in Spain and a fund in Luxembourg, we have all the data in the same place, we apply two different charts of accounts, and that before was a process that was very much manual, and all that has now improved a lot since we implemented it front.

I would say it's a combination of the automation that we can achieve, the centralization by having all the data in one system, flexibility that it allows us to deal with. When you put it all together, it really, I think it enhances the level of quality that we can offer to clients. The advice I would give for anybody that is looking for a similar transformation is to first of all look at their operations and try to see where technology can add value. This needs to be discussed with all the stakeholders and clients also, see where the pain points are and also where you can improve in terms of efficiency and in terms of quality.

And that's actually how we came up to choose the front.
