My name is Kyriakos Basinas, and I'm currently the IT Fund Admin Workstream Manager in Alter Domus. Alter Domus is currently one of the largest fund and corporate service providers dedicated to international private equity clients, debt managers, infrastructure, and real estate firms and private clients. We currently have 39 offices and desks across all five continents. We have been with eFront for many years. We started with eFront Invest Classic version, and we recently upgraded to eFront Invest Version. With eFront invest, we try to apply best practices and standardization in our business process models. And following the upgrade we did last year, we noticed already significant productivity gains.

We have currently the eFront invest module for fund administration, the eFront loan module for depth administration. We have also the eFront Notice module for automatic generation of the notices for our investors. And we have as well the eFront Workflow module to automate internally and actually regulate our business process models. We have also eFront Excel, which allows our users to generate their own reports directly from extracting data from the system. One of the modules that we currently actively using is the eFront Input module, which allows us to quickly onboard the new clients into our platform. The key benefit from the solution for us is that we can automate many of those processes that we have, and we can generate reports on time and accurately for our clients.

Being a strategic client to eFront has significant advantages. We have direct access to the product manager, where we meet regularly. And we share ideas about the new features that we would like eFront to offer us, and I think that this will be beneficial for eFront, Alter Domus, and also for our clients. We are currently actively looking for the eFront Fee module, where we believe that it will give us an additional automation in our fees management. And we are also considering the eFront Consolidation module. If I need to describe eFront with one word, I will say standardization. I believe that if you correctly apply eFront Invest with best practices and standardization, you will see immediately significant productivity gains.
