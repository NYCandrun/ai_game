ROB HERLIHY: My name's Rob Herlihy. I'm a member at IPS Fund Services. We're based in Boston, 17 people, and we're exclusively focused on handling private equity and venture capital fund administration. We've been with eFront now for about 10 years, and when we made the decision, we needed a product that was both flexible and usable. And looking at the competitors, and even more importantly, talking to the clients of competitors, it seemed like eFront had the best value proposition for us in that it gave us the flexibility to build the system we wanted, with the reporting we wanted, and the controls we wanted, as well as being user friendly enough that we could get up and running in a reasonable amount of time.

We use eFront PEO. Like I said, we've been using them for 10 years, and we use them for everything, whether it's CRM, capital call notices, distributions, the GL allocations, pretty much anything we can automate with PEO, we do automate with PEO, and it's been a great solution across our 150 entities. The key benefits we get out of PEO is we have a user-friendly format that allows us to gain many efficiencies, and it makes us faster, more efficient, and more accurate. It's much better to depend on a system for certain things rather than human beings. Before PEO, just creating certain reports would take a lot of manual work, and effort, and spreadsheets, and what have you.

Now, with PEO, we can do a lot of these things by just running a report and reviewing it. So it's been a huge efficiency booster. It's also allowed us to standardize what we do across all our funds. So as we bring in new people, they're trained the same way, and they work with PEO the same way. And having PEO and all the reports set up in a certain fashion or what have you allows us to get people on board, not only with the industry but also our processes, a lot faster than we would otherwise. Our relationship with eFront really began when they bought out our service provider, and the improvement has been tenfold.
