CHRISTOPHER W. MCCHESNEY: I'm Chris McChesney. I'm responsible for Brown Brothers global alternative fund administration business. Brown Brothers is a global fund administrator with approximately $300 billion in client assets across real estate, private equity, private debt, and other liquid strategies. Brown Brothers has been an eFront Invest user for about 10 years, and the use of eFront Invest is incredibly important for us achieving a scalable global operating model and delivering value to global diversified asset managers and premiere specialist managers. Brown Brothers uses Investment Café, so that hundreds of investors can access fund information on a regular basis. We also anticipate using Investment Café to help our clients engage with their investors with dynamic content published regularly to investors.

Investors appreciate having a single communication channel through Investment Café for a variety of fund communications, such as capital calls, distribution notices, and quarterly statements. We've recently acquired eFront's data warehouse. The data warehouse will enable us to provide more advanced reporting to clients, and also enable a new generation of data services from BBH as administrator of alternative funds. The technology we use is extremely important to our clients. eFront helps BBH be a technology leader in the fund administration market. eFront has been a technology partner for BBH for almost 10 years. eFront's customer support team is there for us when we need it. We also get very high quality engagement with eFront's product resources in planning our joint product roadmap.

In short, one word I'd use to describe eFront is capable. eFront has the subject matter expertise in the markets that are most important to us.
