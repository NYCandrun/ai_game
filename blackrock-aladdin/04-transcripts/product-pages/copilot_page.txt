 Introducing Aladdin® Copilot, now available to all Aladdin clients. Through generative AI, Aladdin Copilot serves to strengthen the connective tissue across the Aladdin® platform, surfacing answers instantly to support key business decisions—unlocking new efficiencies and uncovering actionable information faster.
 Aladdin Copilot is making Aladdin more intelligent and responsive—enhancing productivity, enabling scale, and providing ever-more insights within the Aladdin platform.

 Key features

 Harness an intuitive user experience to access immediate answers, empowering every user to be an Aladdin expert

 Unlock new efficiencies through configurable experiences to make the Aladdin platform your own 

 Enable more informed decision making with permission-dependent access to insights
