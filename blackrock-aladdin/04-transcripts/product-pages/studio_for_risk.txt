 Aladdin Studio for Aladdin Risk

 Put risk insights to work across your enterprise

 Aladdin® Studio extends Aladdin Risk beyond the user interface, helping organizations embed risk analytics and data into the systems and workflows where their teams already work. Through open, programmatic access, firms can automate processes, connect risk information to downstream workflows, and build a more scalable risk operating model.

 Modern risk management requires more than analytics
 Aladdin Studio helps organizations operationalize risk across the enterprise by connecting Aladdin Risk analytics in internal processes, reporting environments, internal applications, and decision-making workflows.

 Turn analytics into action
 Connect Aladdin Risk data and analytics directly to the workflows where decisions are made. Programmatic access can help teams automate calculations, integrate outputs into internal systems, and scale analysis across portfolios, securities, and scenarios.

 Scale risk oversight
 Move from manual monitoring to more scalable portfolio oversight. Automate elements of exception monitoring and governance workflows, standardize repeatable processes, and help risk teams focus attention on the issues that require expertise and judgment.

 Connect risk across your technology ecosystem
 Integrate risk information across portfolio systems, proprietary models, reporting environments, and portfolio oversight tools. Teams can continue working in their preferred environments while using a more connected approach to risk management.

 Adapt while maintaining control
 Extend and customize risk workflows as business, investment, and reporting requirements evolve. Standardized, transparent processes can support consistency, accountability, and auditability as the organization grows.

 From insight to execution
 With Aladdin Studio, organizations can:
 Run analytics programmatically: Compute portfolio and security analytics for repeatable reporting, analysis, modeling, and integration into downstream systems.
 Automate portfolio oversight workflows: Support programmatic rules, evaluations, exceptions, escalation, and downstream reporting workflows.
 Build tailored risk solutions: Create risk dashboards, reporting workflows, and custom analytics that combine Aladdin, proprietary, and market data.
 Strengthen data governance and controls: Connect data, workflows, and analytical processes within more transparent, scalable data governance frameworks.

 Build on Aladdin Risk
 Aladdin Studio provides an open, enterprise-ready foundation for integrating Aladdin capabilities into client applications and workflows. Its centralized experience supports the building, configuration, and governance of Studio workflows, including access management and usage monitoring.

 Put your risk insights to work
 Explore how Aladdin Studio can help your organization extend Aladdin Risk, connect risk analytics to the workflows that power decision-making and automate portfolio risk reporting and oversight.
