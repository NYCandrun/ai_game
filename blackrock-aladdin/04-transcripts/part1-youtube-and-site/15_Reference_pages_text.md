 The asset management firm’s tech business discusses how it enables data-driven investment decisions during Snowflake Summit 2024.

 This year’s Snowflake Data Cloud Summit brought together the brightest minds from major enterprises, including leaders from BlackRock’s Aladdin® business — a leading investment management technology provider. I was thrilled to take the stage with Dave Woodhead, BlackRock’s Head of Aladdin Studio Engineering, to share how Fivetran is enabling innovative new products and AI-driven use cases.
 For over 18 years, Woodhead’s team has been pivotal in maintaining a competitive edge for clients through technological innovation. They’re elevating the value of data, making it one of the biggest differentiators in the modern financial environment . 
 The primary vehicle for driving that value is its Aladdin® platform. 
 Aladdin has served as the backbone of BlackRock’s technology and investment operations since the company’s early days, while subsequently serving institutional investors of all types across public and private markets — helping them unify the investment management process. At the summit, Woodhead discussed one of the platform’s recent innovations: Aladdin® Data Cloud (ADC), a next-generation data-as-a-service solution enabled by Fivetran and Snowflake.
 “From data solutions to new tools powered by AI , the Aladdin® platform team is focused on building new innovations on top of the stable foundation of our data strategy,” Woodhead shared. “We expanded Aladdin® Data Cloud’s data network capabilities, enabling clients to bring their own data into ADC, and to distribute Aladdin data to their own data ecosystems.”
 Fivetran and Snowflake help bring near real-time data availability to BlackRock’s Aladdin® Data Cloud
 ADC offers a cloud-based data and analytics platform , allowing users to store, manage, and analyze large volumes of investment and market data. Users can access their Investment Books of Record (IBOR), Accounting Books of Record (ABOR) holdings and other market data — with the ability to ask questions and get quick insights. 
 ADC is supported by Fivetran for data movement and Snowflake as its cloud data platform. “Fivetran allows us to take data from a single source and move that with low latency into a Snowflake landing zone, where we transform it using our enterprise data platform,” says Woodhead. Fivetran proved so powerful that his team was able to build low-latency pipelines into ADC in just a few months. 
 Once the data is in Snowflake, analytics services are provided through APIs for reporting and automation opportunities. From there, users have access to dynamic tables for building Snowpark apps and asking cross-dimensional time-series questions. Answers are provided in near real-time which, according to Woodhead, “wasn’t possible without Fivetran.”
 Fivetran helps address three key issues: disaster recovery, data recoverability, and consolidation of previously siloed data sets into the unified Snowflake environment. 
 Along with other solutions, BlackRock is leveraging Fivetran and Snowflake as part of a larger strategic stack, enhancing everything from trading strategies to even more comprehensive risk management. Aladdin teams are focused on making data available to any client who needs it.
 BlackRock's Aladdin® is a financial technology platform designed for institutional use only and is not intended for end investor use. To learn more about ADC, visit: https://www.blackrock.com/aladdin/products/aladdin-data-cloud

 How BlackRock is finding new alpha as AI Transformation becomes a critical business driver

 At our Microsoft Ignite conference in Chicago, along with over 80 new product announcements, we highlighted the real business impact and results our customers across industries are experiencing through AI Transformation. I had the honor to talk onstage with Lance Braunstein, head of Aladdin Engineering at BlackRock, who joined me to explain BlackRock’s cloud and AI journey.

 Get the AI use cases for business leaders e-book 

 BlackRock’s move to the cloud

 BlackRock’s tech platform, Aladdin, is a pivotal tool for investors. For years BlackRock had been pouring resources into maintaining its data center operations to support it. 

 Five years ago, we began working on a strategy to move them into the cloud on Microsoft Azure . Today, all BlackRock’s technology platform clients and services are hosted in the Azure cloud, and as Lance shared with me onstage, it has been a profound unlock for the firm’s business processes that has allowed them to work more efficiently and get to market quicker. New rollouts now take weeks instead of quarters to complete . Their operations are secured with data encrypted at rest and in transit. 

 With so much of the firm’s success driven by markets, the ability to manage through stress tests like index rebalances is also critical. With Azure, BlackRock is seeing significant improvements in performance during its most demanding moments. 

 The pillars of AI Transformation

 BlackRock has been developing AI solutions for years with predictive analysis, pattern recognition, and machine learning—even starting an AI Lab in 2018—but with the emergence of generative AI there were hundreds of considerations for how they could transform their business. Like most organizations and companies, the big question was where to begin.

 Both internally at Microsoft and with our customers and partners, we have been using four pillars as the framework for AI Transformation to help prioritize projects that will deliver business outcomes.

 Enriching employee experiences : Will you get more productivity out of your workforce? Will you attract better talent because you have the right assets that help people reach their potential faster? Will your employees feel more satisfied because they are eliminating tedious tasks from their day-to-day work?

 Reinventing customer engagement : Will you generate more top-line revenue? Will your customer experiences be better? Will people come back for more as a result of the AI capability you have released?

 Reshaping business processes : Will you improve your operations with AI-first thinking? How can AI technology make a process go faster? Or even better, how can a process be re-designed?

 Bending the curve on innovation : Will you create products faster, get them to market quicker, respond and refine based on customer feedback, and design your offerings in a more differentiated way because of the AI capability that you are implementing?

 My challenge has been that if the scenario does not fall neatly into one of these pillars with strong ROI justification—and projects can certainly span multiple pillars—then you should probably reconsider. This is the frame that we used together with BlackRock.

 AI Transformation at BlackRock

 According to Lance, BlackRock’s employee experience is being transformed in two key areas: one in productivity and the other in supporting investment signal analysis—what is known in the investing world as “alpha.” 

 Identifying the alpha investment signal is all about driving more data into the investment life cycle with richer models that can find the signal in the data. For relationship managers, this translates to finding the right product for a given client’s investment strategy at the right market moment. Driving that signal is supported by Azure Machine Learning and the firm’s foundation models. 

 On productivity, BlackRock believes that every job family can be supported through AI, helping employees with first drafts of an email, a pitch or code. In that spirit, the company has implemented Microsoft 365 Copilot and GitHub Copilot. BlackRock is now reshaping business processes with bespoke workflows for different functions within the business.

 BlackRock is bending the curve on innovation not just using AI internally with Copilot Studio to build agents and copilots while measuring their Azure consumption, but also using tools like GitHub Copilot to help infuse AI into product capabilities . My favorite example of this is the work they are doing to reinvent customer engagement .

 The company has traditionally delivered its investment platforms through a set of user and application programming interfaces. But with the launches of the Aladdin and eFront copilots, they are now delivering a set of natural language interfaces—meaning customers can now have a conversation within an Aladdin environment with responsible AI safeguards built in.

 Imagine asking your investment software questions like: “What is my exposure to companies building AI processors?” Before, this may have required a lot of searching and ultimately a phone call to the advisor. The ability to ask the Aladdin platform specific operational questions makes inquiries to support investment decisions much more efficient and satisfying. BlackRock technology clients are already using this capability today. 

 Co-innovation with our customers and partners

 I am grateful for Microsoft’s partnership with BlackRock, and Lance and I believe that both Azure and Aladdin are stronger for the collaboration. 

 BlackRock has pushed us to make Azure better for all our customers around the world and that helped us successfully take the Aladdin offerings to market. Microsoft is fueling BlackRock’s internal transformation with solutions like Dynamics 365, customer relationship management business applications, and copilots to improve the work experience for their employees and customers.

 Microsoft is co-innovating with thousands of the world’s most forward-thinking organizations , helping them realize the value and ROI AI is delivering today.

 As companies embrace their own AI Transformation, we are here to help. Recently we’ve launched new resources to help business leaders explore some of the most common and impactful AI use cases , and to help organizations assess their AI readiness .

 Aladdin Studio for Aladdin Risk

 Put risk insights to work across your enterprise

 Aladdin® Studio extends Aladdin Risk beyond the user interface, helping organizations embed risk analytics and data into the systems and workflows where their teams already work. Through open, programmatic access, firms can automate processes, connect risk information to downstream workflows, and build a more scalable risk operating model.

 Modern risk management requires more than analytics
 Aladdin Studio helps organizations operationalize risk across the enterprise by connecting Aladdin Risk analytics in internal processes, reporting environments, internal applications, and decision-making workflows.

 Turn analytics into action
 Connect Aladdin Risk data and analytics directly to the workflows where decisions are made. Programmatic access can help teams automate calculations, integrate outputs into internal systems, and scale analysis across portfolios, securities, and scenarios.

 Scale risk oversight
 Move from manual monitoring to more scalable portfolio oversight. Automate elements of exception monitoring and governance workflows, standardize repeatable processes, and help risk teams focus attention on the issues that require expertise and judgment.

 Connect risk across your technology ecosystem
 Integrate risk information across portfolio systems, proprietary models, reporting environments, and portfolio oversight tools. Teams can continue working in their preferred environments while using a more connected approach to risk management.

 Adapt while maintaining control
 Extend and customize risk workflows as business, investment, and reporting requirements evolve. Standardized, transparent processes can support consistency, accountability, and auditability as the organization grows.

 From insight to execution
 With Aladdin Studio, organizations can:
 Run analytics programmatically: Compute portfolio and security analytics for repeatable reporting, analysis, modeling, and integration into downstream systems.
 Automate portfolio oversight workflows: Support programmatic rules, evaluations, exceptions, escalation, and downstream reporting workflows.
 Build tailored risk solutions: Create risk dashboards, reporting workflows, and custom analytics that combine Aladdin, proprietary, and market data.
 Strengthen data governance and controls: Connect data, workflows, and analytical processes within more transparent, scalable data governance frameworks.

 Build on Aladdin Risk
 Aladdin Studio provides an open, enterprise-ready foundation for integrating Aladdin capabilities into client applications and workflows. Its centralized experience supports the building, configuration, and governance of Studio workflows, including access management and usage monitoring.

 Explore how Aladdin Studio can help your organization extend Aladdin Risk, connect risk analytics to the workflows that power decision-making and automate portfolio risk reporting and oversight.

 Aladdin Wealth™ Launches AI-Enabled Commentary Tool for Wealth Advisors; Morgan Stanley’s Portfolio Risk Platform First to Implement

 Morgan Stanley Wealth Management will continue its GenAI leadership position by deploying the Aladdin Wealth platform’s new AI tool in the U.S. within their Portfolio Risk Platform.

 NEW YORK – 2 October 2025 – Aladdin Wealth ™, wealth management technology from BlackRock, is introducing Gen AI-driven functionalities designed to help financial advisors deliver more personalized, insightful, and efficient client service. The new “Auto Commentary” feature turns complex portfolio analytics and client-level investment preferences into concise insights, helping financial advisors craft relevant narratives to deepen client relationships by delivering more meaningful, differentiated conversations.
 Auto Commentary brings together three powerful inputs: the Aladdin Wealth platform’s robust data and risk analytics, an individual firm’s CIO market outlook, and detailed information about the client’s portfolio holdings and investment preferences. Leveraging the power of Gen AI, Auto Commentary assesses hundreds of data points and summarizes them in a way that highlights the most relevant insights. Armed with these insights, financial advisors can deliver personalized service at scale, differentiating their value and increasing their impact to drive growth across clients.
 “The opportunity to transform wealth management through technology has never been greater,” said Ted Stratigos, Global Head of Aladdin Wealth Tech. “To meet this moment, we’re harnessing the power of AI to simplify complex workflows and enhance how financial advisors connect with clients, driving greater efficiency and deepening financial advisor-client engagement. At BlackRock, we’re committed to staying ahead of client needs by using AI as a catalyst for meaningful innovation.”
 Morgan Stanley’s proprietary Portfolio Risk Platform, which leverages a combination of BlackRock’s Aladdin risk analytics and Morgan Stanley data, will be the first to implement this new AI-powered capability. Starting in October, financial advisors will begin accessing Auto Commentary to bring AI innovation to the forefront of their client experience, enabling them to spend more time on critical client conversations.
 "Auto Commentary leverages AI to integrate hundreds of analytics and firm-level market insights into a cohesive narrative," said Chris Scott-Hansen, Managing Director and Head of Consulting Group at Morgan Stanley Wealth Management. "By automating labor-intensive research, we enable our financial advisors to concentrate on what truly matters—building trust through assisting clients in navigating complex market dynamics. Morgan Stanley is committed to implementing technology solutions that modernize the future of advice: less time spent gathering data, more time delivering actionable insights to every client."

 Frequently asked questions

 Which types of institutions benefit from Whole Portfolio’s multi-asset solutions? 

 Whole Portfolio is designed for asset managers, banks, insurers, pension plans, corporations, and ecosystem partners. Its flexible architecture and scalable infrastructure support a wide range of investment strategies and operational needs.

 What types of risk management solutions are built into the Whole Portfolio platform? 

 Whole Portfolio includes proprietary risk models, stress testing tools, pacing analysis, and real-time analytics. It supports holistic risk assessment across public and private assets, enabling teams to quantify exposures and make informed decisions.

 Can Aladdin work with my existing systems and data providers? 

 Aladdin is an open, API-first platform that adapts to your code and strategy across the investment management process. The platform offers multi-asset solutions and an ecosystem of partners across data vendors, systems integrators, and liquidity providers—providing clients with openness across multiple dimensions to drive flexibility, extensibility, and interoperability.

 How does Whole Portfolio by Aladdin support investment management across public and private markets? 

 Whole Portfolio enables seamless management of both public and private assets by integrating data, workflows, and analytics into a single ecosystem. It models private market cashflows natively and combines them with public market data, allowing for unified oversight and comparability across asset classes.

 How does Whole Portfolio help with asset allocation and portfolio management across multiple asset classes? 

 Whole Portfolio provides a single integrated view of all holdings, enabling precise asset allocation and portfolio rebalancing. It supports real-time monitoring, historical snapshots, and enterprise-level oversight, helping teams act decisively across diverse strategies.

 What does it mean to have a “single integrated view” in portfolio allocation and risk oversight? 

 A single integrated view means that all asset classes—public and private—are visible in one system, allowing for consistent analysis, governance, and decision-making. This eliminates data silos and ensures that risk and performance are assessed holistically across the portfolio.
